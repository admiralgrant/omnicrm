Privacy & AI Disclosure

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

OmniCRM ("we", "us") provides a customer relationship and business operations platform for Nigerian businesses. Each organization that subscribes runs its own workspace. The organization's owner controls the account, its staff, and its data.

2. Data we process

We store the data your organization enters: customers and contacts (names, phone numbers, email addresses), products and inventory levels, invoices and payments, support tickets, staff records, payroll details, and team chat messages. We do not sell personal data. We do not use your data to train third-party models.

3. AI assistance and how it works

OmniCRM includes AI-assisted features: follow-up message drafts, WhatsApp reply drafts, activity reports, and inventory digests. These are tools that suggest content. A human always reviews and approves before anything is sent to a customer. AI never sends messages, charges money, or changes records on its own.

AI outputs may be generated by rule-based logic running on our servers, or, when enabled, by a third-party language model provider. When a language model is used, the minimum information needed to draft a reply (contact name, conversation context, and record state) is sent to that provider for processing. Providers are selected with data protection agreements where available, and prompts containing customer data are not retained by us.

4. WhatsApp and messaging

Messages exchanged through the WhatsApp inbox belong to the organization. The organization, not the platform, decides who may view and reply to conversations. Department access rules apply: staff see only what their role permits.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by licensed payment providers. OmniCRM's wallet is a ledger of your balances and transactions; actual funds are held and moved by the licensed processor, never by us.

6. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. Contact the organization that collected your data, or email privacy@omnicrm.ng and we will direct the request appropriately.

7. Security

Access is role-based: staff, department heads, and administrators see only what their role permits. Passwords are stored hashed. All actions in vendor-support mode are logged in an audit trail.

8. Changes

We update this notice as the product evolves. Material changes will be announced in-product.
